# RouteMax Logistics: Claims SOP (v3, last updated March 2025)

Applies to damage and shortage claims from customers.

1. **Time limit.** Claims must be filed within 7 days of delivery. Late claims are rejected.
2. **Evidence.** Damage claims must include photos of the damaged goods. If photos are missing, ask the customer for them before deciding.
3. **Proof of delivery (POD).** If the customer signed the POD as "received in good condition" with no remarks, damage claims are rejected. Shortage claims are rejected if the POD confirms the full piece count.
4. **Shortage.** Approve the shortage if the POD remarks show fewer pieces than were shipped.
5. **Maximum payout.** We never pay more than the declared value of the shipment.
6. **Approval limit.** Claims above Rs 50,000 must be approved by the Claims Manager (Meera).
7. **Replies.** Every customer gets a reply that states the decision and the reason.
